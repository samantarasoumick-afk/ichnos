Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-07-26.

Early access notice: DatFe is currently in early access. This policy accurately describes how the product works today. It has not yet been reviewed by outside legal counsel, and will be before we take on paying customers processing production personal data at scale. If you're evaluating DatFe for a use case involving regulated personal data (DPDP, GDPR, or similar), have your own counsel review this alongside us before go-live.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers (a) visitors to our marketing site, and (b) organizations and individual users of the DatFe application ("the Service"). If you're an end user invited by your employer to use a self-hosted DatFe instance, your employer is the data controller for that instance — this policy describes our handling of data on our own hosted infrastructure, and our contractual obligations to any organization we host on behalf of.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and hashed password (or magic-link email if you use passwordless login).
  • Organization data: the metadata you or your teammates enter directly — dataset descriptions, ownership assignments, glossary terms, governance notes, discussion threads.
  • Connected source metadata: when you register a data source (database, warehouse, file), DatFe reads schema metadata (table/column names, types, relationships) and stores a limited number of sample values per column for catalog search and preview purposes. We do not ingest your full underlying datasets.
  • Usage data: logins, dataset views, and actions taken in the app, used for the audit log feature and basic operational logging.

3. How we use it

To operate the Service (authentication, cataloging, search, governance workflows), to maintain the audit trail your organization's admins can see, and to improve the product. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your organization's cataloged data to train any model.

4. Third parties

If your organization enables the AI "Ask" assistant with an Anthropic API key, relevant catalog context and your question text are sent to Anthropic's API to generate an answer. This is opt-in per organization. We don't share your data with any other third party except as needed to operate core infrastructure (e.g. hosting), or as required by law.

5. Data retention & deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account or organization is active. On request, we'll delete an organization's data within a reasonable period, subject to any residual copies in backups, which are purged on our normal backup rotation schedule.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under GDPR and India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights.
