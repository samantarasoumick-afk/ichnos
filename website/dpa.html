Legal

Data Processing Agreement

Last updated 2026-07-26.

Early access notice: This DPA describes our processing role and safeguards as they exist today. It has not been reviewed by outside counsel and is not yet signed/executable as a standalone legal instrument — treat it as a transparent statement of intent. If your organization requires a fully executed DPA (common under GDPR Article 28 or similar), contact us and we'll work through a signed version together before you send production personal data through the Service.

1. Roles

When your organization uses DatFe to catalog data that includes personal data, your organization is the data controller and DatFe acts as the data processor, processing personal data only on your organization's documented instructions (as expressed through your use of the product).

2. Subject matter, duration, and purpose

Subject matterMetadata cataloging, classification, lineage tracking, and governance workflows over data sources you connect to DatFe.
DurationFor as long as your organization maintains an active account, plus the retention/deletion window described in our Privacy Policy.
PurposeProviding the Service: discovery, cataloging, quality profiling, lineage, and governance features you and your team use directly.

3. Categories of data & data subjects

Depending on what you connect, this may include: schema metadata (which is generally not personal data itself), a limited number of sample values per column (which may include personal data if present in your source systems, e.g. customer names or emails used as illustrative samples), and account data for your own team members (name, email). Data subjects may include your customers, employees, or other individuals whose data appears in systems you connect.

4. Sub-processors

Current sub-processor: Anthropic (only if your organization opts in to the AI "Ask" assistant with your own API key - question text and relevant catalog context is sent to Anthropic's API). We'll notify you before adding any new sub-processor that would process personal data on our behalf.

5. Security measures

See our Trust & Security page for the current, honest state of technical and organizational measures (encryption at rest for source credentials, tenant isolation, RBAC, audit logging) and what's still open (SSO, third-party pen test, SOC 2).

6. Assisting with data subject rights

We'll support your organization in responding to data subject access, correction, or deletion requests to the extent the relevant data is stored in DatFe, within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Deletion on termination

On termination of your account, we'll delete your organization's data within a reasonable period, subject to residual copies in backups purged on our normal rotation schedule - consistent with our Privacy Policy.

8. International transfers

If your data is processed in a jurisdiction different from where it originates, we'll rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) as required by applicable law. Exact hosting location is disclosed on request - during early access, this may be a self-hosted or personally-managed instance rather than a fixed cloud region.
